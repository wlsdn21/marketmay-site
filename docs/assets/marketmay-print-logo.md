# 인쇄용 마켓메이 로고

- 사용자 요청: 검게 보이는 메뉴판 로고를 이미지 생성으로 새로 만들고 흰 A4 바탕에 배치.
- 생성 방식: 내장 이미지 생성 도구. 원본 메뉴판 사진의 작은 로고를 참고.
- 파일: `marketmay-print-logo.png`. 흰 배경, 짙은 초록색 상호와 산 모양, 작은 아치형 창문.
- 사용처: A4 메뉴판의 기존 로고 위치. 비율을 유지하고 메뉴나 가격 위에 겹치지 않음.

## 생성 프롬프트

Use case: logo-brand. Create a NEW clean production logo asset for the Korean cafe MARKET MAY, inspired by the tiny logo on the LEFT menu sheet of the reference photograph (near the middle-right of that left sheet). The photograph is an identity reference only, NOT the output. Output ONE isolated flat logo on a pure solid white #FFFFFF background, in a wide horizontal composition about 2.4:1. Exact text, once only: "MARKET MAY" in clear widely spaced uppercase geometric sans-serif type, medium weight. Below the text, retain the essence of the existing simple angular mountain ridgeline / gable roof motif with several low peaks, and one tiny arched window under a peak. Reinterpret as crisp elegant medium-weight deep forest-green (#28553C) linework and green lettering on WHITE. There must be NO dark filled rectangle, NO black background, NO beige, no paper photograph or paper texture, no shadow, no gradient, no border around the logo, no mockup, no cafe scene, no menu, no pricing, no QR code, no other text. The mark must be light, airy but legible when printed about 35 mm wide. Keep line weights strong enough for small-format print. The logo should occupy about 85% of the wide canvas, with modest equal white margins. Deliver a high-resolution clean raster logo.
